Materials & Specification

Architectural modelmaking meets heirloom cabinetry.

Every Hearthscale piece combines multi-jet full-color photopolymer fabrication with kiln-dried American black walnut and solid brushed C260 cartridge brass.

SPEC 01

25-Micron Full-Color UV PolyJet Resin

Unlike monochrome filament prints, full-color photopolymer resin reproduces terracotta barrel tiles, stacked canyon stone, window mullions, solar panels, and translucent turquoise pool water directly within the cured matrix.

SPEC 02

Solid American Black Walnut Plinth

Milled from FAS-grade kiln-dried American black walnut with chamfered museum edges, hand-sanded to 400 grit, and finished in satin natural oil with recessed velvet desk pads underneath.

SPEC 03

Dual Laser-Engraved Brushed Brass Plates

Precision fiber-laser annealed lettering on solid brushed brass — crisp serif address inscription on the front bevel, paired with your private referral dedication recessed into the underside.
